What is automation?

Automation means using software to carry out repetitive tasks by following rules you define, instead of doing them by hand. For a business that can mean replying to enquiries, copying data between tools, sending reminders or creating CRM records. A good workflow also includes checks and human review where a mistake would be costly.

What is the difference between AI automation and an AI agent?

AI automation follows a fixed workflow designed in advance. The steps and their order are set, and an AI model may handle one step, such as classifying a message or drafting a reply. An AI agent is given a goal and decides for itself which steps or tools to use, so its path can vary. Automations are more predictable and easier to test. Agents are more flexible but need strict limits and human oversight. Many business problems are best solved with a well-defined automation, with AI added only where it helps.

Are an LLM and an AI agent the same thing?

No. A large language model (LLM) is the underlying model that understands and generates text, such as the model behind a chatbot. An AI agent is a system built around an LLM. It adds instructions, tools such as APIs, databases or a calendar, memory, and a loop that lets it plan and act towards a goal. An LLM on its own only produces text, while an agent uses an LLM to decide on and carry out actions.

What is the WhatsApp API?

The WhatsApp API usually means the WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. It lets a business send and receive WhatsApp messages from software, so enquiries can be answered, reminders sent and conversations connected to tools such as a CRM. Access comes through Meta or an approved provider, customers must opt in to receive messages, and message templates and fees follow WhatsApp’s current rules. It is different from the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app, which people use by hand.
