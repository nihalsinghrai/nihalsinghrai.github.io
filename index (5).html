Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before starting an automation project.

Who is Nihal Singh Rai?

I’m an automation developer moving into AI engineering. I build workflows that connect business tools through their APIs, so enquiries, leads and routine steps don’t depend on copy and paste. My current work is WhatsApp enquiry automation for clinics, and I’m learning where AI steps genuinely help.

I run a business outside India. What problems can you solve for me?

A common one is enquiries arriving in several places at once, such as WhatsApp, email, a web form and a spreadsheet. I connect them so every new enquiry lands in one record, gets a prompt reply and reaches the right person. I can also link tools you already use, like a CRM or calendar, where they offer API access. We can plan the work over email or a call, whatever suits your time zone.

My business is in India. What problems can you solve for me?

Clinics, salons, coaching centres and local service businesses often lose enquiries that arrive on WhatsApp during busy hours or late at night. I can set up a workflow that replies straight away, saves the customer’s details to a Google Sheet, alerts your staff, and books a calendar slot only after someone approves it. WhatsApp messaging charges and any paid tools are separate, and I’ll show them before we start.

What kind of businesses can use your automation services?

Clinics, salons, service businesses and small teams can benefit from workflows that manage enquiries, appointments, lead records, reminders and routine handoffs. The right solution depends on the process and tools already in use.

Can you automate WhatsApp enquiries and appointment requests?

Yes. A workflow can capture an incoming enquiry, collect required details, update a connected sheet or CRM, notify staff and send a response. Appointment confirmation can include a human approval step where needed.

Does every automation need AI?

No. Rule-based automation is often more predictable, easier to test and less expensive to maintain. AI is useful only when a task genuinely needs language understanding, classification or drafting.

Can you connect Google Sheets, Google Calendar or a CRM?

Yes, where the relevant service provides suitable API access and permissions. Before development, I check the available integrations, account requirements and any paid-plan restrictions.

How do you prevent duplicate bookings or failed runs?

Workflows can check existing records and calendar availability before creating a booking. They can also include validation, error branches, alerts and a clear escalation path. The exact safeguards are designed around the business process.

How long does an automation project take?

It depends on the number of steps, integrations, approval rules and testing requirements. A small workflow may be straightforward; a multi-system booking process needs more discovery and testing. I confirm the scope before estimating delivery.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Describe the current process, the problem you want to solve and the apps you use. If the workflow needs WhatsApp or another third-party service, the business may need to provide its own account access, API permissions or subscription.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Support and maintenance can be discussed as part of the project scope. Any ongoing hosting, API usage or third-party subscription costs should be identified separately before implementation.
